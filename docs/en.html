BEFORE DEPARTURE

The quantity exemption does not remove all obligations.

01 / CLASSIFICATION

Check the input data

A UN number does not always identify one transport entry. Check the name, packing group, classification code and physical state against the consignor information. Use quantities for the complete transport unit.

02 / PACKAGES

Check packaging and loading

Follow the requirements for packaging, marks, labels and load securing. Check mixed-loading prohibitions (7.5.2), separation from food (7.5.4), and handling provisions (7.5.11).

03 / EQUIPMENT

Check documents and training

Even under the quantity exemption, applicable transport documents, task-specific training and a fire extinguisher of at least 2 kg are required. Check exceptions for supervision and security measures. Source: 1.1.3.6.2, 1.3, 5.4.1, 8.1.4.2 and 8.2.3.

How does the calculation work?

Category 1: quantity × 50. Footnote a substances: quantity × 20 (UN 0081, 0082, 0084, 0241, 0331, 0332, 0482, 1005, 1017). Category 2: quantity × 3. Category 3: quantity × 1. Category 4 adds zero. A category 0 substance prevents use of the quantity exemption.

Single-category limits are 20, 50 (footnote a), 333 and 1,000. The calculated value for a mixed load must not exceed 1,000. The value is not a sum of kilograms and litres. Calculation: 1.1.3.6.3–1.1.3.6.4.

Limited quantities (LQ) and excepted quantities (EQ)

LQ (3.4) and EQ (3.5) are separate schemes. The table LQ value applies to the inner packaging. It is not the load quantity exemption. This calculator does not approve LQ or EQ exemptions automatically. Check packaging, marks, package mass and other transport conditions separately. Do not omit substances without a confirmed exemption.

Other transport cases and restrictions to check

Check empty uncleaned packaging, waste, damaged batteries, temperature control, radioactive material, explosives security measures, local transport and bus transport separately. The calculator does not check substance compatibility, tunnel routes or national route restrictions.

The package exemption calculated here does not apply to tank or bulk transport. Assess sea, ferry, rail and air transport separately. Also check Annex C of the VAK regulation.
